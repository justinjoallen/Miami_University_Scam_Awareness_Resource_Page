# Miami_University_Scam_Awareness_Resource_Page

This project is part of a student-led initiative to support Miami University students in identifying and avoiding job‑search scams, especially those appearing on Handshake or through email. The goal is to provide a clear, accessible, and student‑friendly resource that breaks down common red flags, offers real examples, and connects students with the correct Miami support offices.

This is the first iteration of the Scam Awareness FAQ page, created for review by:
- Center for Career Exploration & Success (Oxford)
- Career Services & Professional Development (Regionals)
- CIT 268 students (usability feedback)

---

##  Purpose of the Project

Students across Miami’s campuses regularly encounter fake job postings, phishing emails, and scam internship offers. This page is designed to:

- Help students quickly evaluate whether a job or message is legitimate  
- Provide examples of common scam tactics  
- Offer clear steps for reporting suspicious activity  
- Connect students with the correct Miami University resources  
- Improve student safety and confidence during the job search process  

This resource will eventually be part of a larger Scam Awareness Hub that includes:
- A digital FAQ (this page)
- A physical handout with a QR code
- A short informational video
- A feedback loop with Career Services and CIT 268 students

---
